A woman's near-death experience shatters her marriage, her career, and everything she thought she knew about reality. *Five Minutes on the Low Road* explores what happens when someone returns from clinical death with radically reset priorities, and certainty that consciousness survives — to a world that isn't ready to hear her. Book, music, and lyrics by NMTE member Charles Betz; additional book, lyrics, and music by Josiah Thomas Turner; orchestrations by Ben Jossi. Selections were workshopped at Nautilus Music-Theater's Rough Cuts in March 2026.
