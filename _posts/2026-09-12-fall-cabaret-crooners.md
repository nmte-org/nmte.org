On **Monday, October 12**, the New Musical Theatre Exchange returned to the **Dunsmore Jazz Room** at <a href="https://www.croonersmn.com/" target="_blank">Crooners Supper Club</a> for its **2026 Fall Cabaret**: ten new musicals, eleven NMTE writers, and a stage full of the Twin Cities' best singers, all in one evening, in front of a sold-out house. Thank you to everyone who came out, and to the writers, performers, and musicians who made it happen.

The evening opened with a cafe meet-cute from Maureen Kane Berg and Tom Berg's office-politics-and-true-crime comedy *Got It Made*, with Josh Zapata-Palmer and Therese Kulas as Freddy and Chloe. Nick Miller and Elisheva Scheuer brought Kevin and Lynn Bowen's *Foshay!* to Crooners as Wilbur and Leota Foshay, including the first performance of "Time Is A Gift" as a duet. Michael Burton, who sang three very different characters over the night, began as Mario, the 1920 Wall Street bomber in Keith Benson's *Anzhelina & Karina*, sung to Eric Heltemes's prerecorded orchestrations.

Brenda Varda's *The Small, Small Time* took us to a Los Angeles theatre in 1917, with Andrew Schufman as owner Harry Cowles and Maya Pantanowitz as the singer Estelle Watson. Bob Derus picked up his guitar for *AGASSIZ!*, singing with Joyce Lyons about caring for a warming Earth and the ways water protects life. Mariénne Kreitlow played her own score for *Excelsius*, with Michael Burton as Nestor and Nicole A. Korbisch as Ieda, and Martha Fischer accompanied Kiran Arquin and Maya Pantanowitz as new parents in Rick Rhobajt Widen's *Socks & Shoes & Coats*.

Joe and Mark McCullough brought Mike Nilles's high-school rock musical *It's Just the Rest of My Life!*, with a brass arrangement by Bill Kelm. Jen Burleigh-Bentz and Jake Endres sang Tanya and the mysterious Uncle in Charlie Betz's *Five Minutes On The Low Road*. And Michael Burton closed the show in full wig as the Troll Ma in *Snip, Snap, Snute: The Real Story of the Three Billy Goats Gruff* by Janet Preus, Margi Preus, and Dieter Frank. Brenda Varda accompanied four of the evening's ten pieces.

### Program

**"Cappuccino and Tiramisu" and "Let's Be Each Other's Problem"** from *Got It Made* — Maureen Kane Berg and Tom Berg; featuring Josh Zapata-Palmer (Freddy) and Therese Kulas (Chloe); Brenda Varda, piano

**"Name In Lights" and "Time Is A Gift"** from *Foshay!* — Kevin and Lynn Bowen, original concept by Tim Colby; featuring Nick Miller (Wilbur Foshay) and Elisheva Scheuer (Leota Foshay)

**"My Dearest Friends," "100 Pounds of Dynamite," and "100 Pounds of Dynamite, Reprise"** from *Anzhelina & Karina* — Keith Benson, prerecorded orchestrations by Eric Heltemes; featuring Michael Burton (Mario)

**"What A Splendid Story From Your Dreams"** from *The Small, Small Time* — Brenda Varda; featuring Andrew Schufman (Harry Cowles) and Maya Pantanowitz (Estelle Watson); Brenda Varda, piano

**"One Blue World" and "Rap't in Water"** from *AGASSIZ!* — Bob Derus; featuring Bob Derus (Jonny) and Joyce Lyons (Emily)

**"Nestor's Vigil" and "Goodbye, Rio"** from *Excelsius* — Mariénne Kreitlow; featuring Michael Burton (Nestor) and Nicole A. Korbisch (Ieda); Mariénne Kreitlow, piano

**"Late Coming Back" and "All My Love & My Good Intentions"** from *Socks & Shoes & Coats* — Rick Rhobajt Widen; featuring Kiran Arquin (Partner) and Maya Pantanowitz (Spouse); Martha Fischer, piano

**"So What!" and "Hanging Over Me"** from *It's Just the Rest of My Life!* — Mike Nilles, brass arrangement by Bill Kelm; featuring Joe McCullough (Mr. Hatch/Friend 2) and Mark McCullough (Alec/Friend 1)

**"Earth Was The Dream" and "This Side Of Tomorrow"** from *Five Minutes On The Low Road* — Charlie Betz, additional music & lyrics and dramaturgy by Josiah Thomas-Turner; featuring Jen Burleigh-Bentz (Tanya) and Jake Endres (Uncle); Brenda Varda, piano

**"Troll Ma's Confessional"** from *Snip, Snap, Snute: The Real Story of the Three Billy Goats Gruff* — Janet Preus, Margi Preus, and Dieter Frank; featuring Michael Burton (Troll Ma); Brenda Varda, piano
