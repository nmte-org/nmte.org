The New Musical Theatre Exchange returned to <a href="https://www.croonersmn.com/" target="_blank">Crooners Supper Club</a> on **Monday, October 12** for its **2026 Fall Cabaret** -- an evening of the Twin Cities' best new musical theatre works, curated by the area's oldest musical theatre writers collective. Thank you to everyone who came out, and to the writers, performers, and musicians who made it happen.

The show took place in Crooners' intimate 87-seat **Dunsmore Jazz Room**.

### New Music By

Brenda Varda · Maureen Kane Berg · Tom Berg · Kevin Bowen · Janet Preus · Marienne Kreitlow · Bob Derus · Mike Nilles · Rick Rhobajt Widen · Keith Benson · Charles Betz

### Guest Performers

Nicole Korbisch · Kiran Arquin · Maya Pantanowitz · Michael Burton · Josh Zapata-Palmer · Therese Kulas · Jake Endres · Jen Burleigh-Bentz · Andrew Schufman on vocals, with **Martha Fischer** and **Brenda Varda** accompanying.
