NMTE member **R.D. Rhobajt** brings a new musical, *Socks & Shoes & Coats*, to the **2026 Minnesota Fringe Festival** this August at the **Barbara Barker Center for Dance**.

The show follows new parents navigating "pregnancy & pediatricians & preschool & poop" — somewhere in the realm of classic two-handers like *The Fantasticks* or *I Do! I Do!* It features **8 new songs** and, in a signature Rhobajt touch, **captioned violin baby-talk**. Written and produced by Rick and directed by **John Widen**, it's a comedy with music suitable for audiences roughly ages 7 and up.

This Fringe staging is an abridged version; Rick is developing the full-length show — follow along and support it at [rhobajt.com/socks](https://rhobajt.com/socks){:target="_blank"}.
