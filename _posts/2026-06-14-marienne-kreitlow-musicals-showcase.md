### Yours, Thrognog

Montage from a partially-staged production, Theatre & Dance Dept. at WIU. "Signing off with his quirky 'Yours, Thrognog,' Uncle Burt's letters to his beloved Doris during World War II chronicle an intimate glimpse into a budding relationship in a tumultuous time." Interfaced with present time, the character of elderly Doris conveys how dementia allows reality to morph into a personal healing mythology. We also meet a granddaughter desperately seeking direction and forgiveness. Boundaries of space and time grow increasingly mutable. Lake Superior cradles the story beginning to end. Book by Kitty Karn & Marienne Kreitlow, music & lyrics by Marienne.

### Tesla's Mistress

Highlights from *Tesla's Mistress*, including an interview with creator Marienne Kreitlow. Set in America during an era of endless innovation when men competed for patent rights, money, power, and fame, Tesla lost at playing that game. *Tesla's Mistress* exposes his charming charisma, obsessive genius, naive confidence, and fatal flaws — seen through the eyes of the women who loved him. Reading hosted by NMTE. Lyrics, music, and script by Marienne Kreitlow, orchestration by Eric Heltemes.
